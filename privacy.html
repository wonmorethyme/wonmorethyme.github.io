Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Strata Agents is a private application used only by Elemental Energy, LLC and people it authorizes. It accesses data in services the account owner connects, such as Google Drive and Intuit QuickBooks (QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Time, and QuickBooks Payments), solely to perform tasks requested by authorized users.

Data is processed on the owner's own server. OAuth tokens and app keys are stored with restricted file permissions on that server, and each tool call is logged for auditing. No Google or Intuit user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models. Full card and bank numbers are never retrieved.

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for QuickBooks under Settings > Apps in QuickBooks (choose Disconnect).

See also the End-User License Agreement.
