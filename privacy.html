Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Strata Agents is a private application used only by its owner. It accesses Google user data (such as Google Drive files) solely to perform tasks requested by the owner.

Data is processed on the owner's own server. OAuth tokens are stored with restricted file permissions on that server. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/wonmorethyme/wonmorethyme.github.io/issues
